## How to sign

We use a lightweight sign-off model. By adding a `Signed-off-by` line to your
commits you certify the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/)
and agree to this CLA:

```
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your.email@example.com>
```

Add it automatically with:

```bash
git commit -s -m "your message"
```

Alternatively, 4Science may ask you to confirm agreement to this CLA on your
first pull request.
